Artifact 01

CareerPath AI Chatbot

An offline chatbot prototype designed to support students, recent graduates, and job seekers with career preparation.

Purpose

CareerPath helps users practice interviews, structure STAR responses, prepare study plans, improve resumes, and think through workplace situations.

Features explored

  • Mock interview questions
  • STAR interview-answer practice
  • Five-day interview preparation plan
  • Resume improvement guidance
  • Workplace disagreement scenarios

Testing and evidence

The chatbot was reported as working during initial testing. Detailed test records and screenshots can be added here when available.

Live artifact: Open the existing CareerPath page ↗

Reflection

This project helped me explore how a focused chatbot can guide users through practical career-preparation tasks. Future improvements could include more personalized feedback and a more structured evaluation process.
