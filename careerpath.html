CareerPath AI Chatbot

An offline career-preparation chatbot for students, graduates, and job seekers.

What it does

  • Mock interview questions and STAR answer practice
  • Five-day interview preparation plans
  • Resume improvement suggestions
  • Workplace disagreement scenarios

My learning

CareerPath is my practical exploration of designing an AI-assisted career support experience. The project has been used for the scenarios listed above; I have not published quantitative performance claims.
