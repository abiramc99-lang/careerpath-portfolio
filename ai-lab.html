AI Lab Experiments

Hands-on learning with AI tools and structured prompting.

Explorations

I compared responses from ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to similar questions about the future of AI agents. I also explored follow-up prompts, summarization, Custom GPT interactions, STORM brainstorming and reports, and chatbot design thinking.

What I observed

Although some answers were similar at first glance, the agents differed in wording, reasoning emphasis, and presentation. Summarization was useful for extracting key ideas while reducing repetition.
