AI Lab Experiments

Comparing AI assistants and exploring how prompts influence responses.

Three-agent comparison

I asked ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini the same question about how they expected to change over five years. Their answers had similar themes, but I noticed differences in their reasoning and presentation.

Follow-up experiment

I asked what would happen if another AI assistant became better in the future. I also explored how the assistants summarized the conversation and removed unnecessary explanation.

What I learned

Different AI tools can approach the same task in noticeably different ways. Comparing them helped me pay closer attention to clarity, reasoning, and response style.
